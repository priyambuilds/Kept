A K that is also a check mark.
Every concept stays in the app's language: near-black, one lime accent, heavy Geist. They're ranked by how well they say "you kept your word" at 29px on a home screen. I recommend 1a.
1aThe Check-KRECOMMENDED
Where the K's arms meet, a small check mark sits in the joint. The letter is the verdict: KEPT. It works in one colour, reads at 16px, and the lime check is the same mark the app uses for a kept day.
1bThe Stake Coin
The Check-K stamped on the coin the Keeper flips. It says money is at stake, but it turns into a generic token icon fast. Better as a secondary mark for payouts and share cards.
1cThe Seal
A stamped seal, like an oath signed in wax. It suits the ritual well, but the scalloped edge gets mushy below 40px and feels a bit like a certificate badge.
1dThe Tally
Prison-wall tally marks counting kept days. It fits the warden side of the Keeper, but it reads as "five" or "counting" before it reads as "kept", and there's no letter in it.
